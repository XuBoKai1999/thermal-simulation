# ADR01 0–1 s timestep comparison

## Scope

Compared `dt=0.1`, `0.05`, and `0.025 s` over the unchanged ADR01 `0–1 s`
problem. All runs used common output times `t=0.1, 0.2, ..., 1.0 s`. No physics,
mesh, solver, or boundary-condition code was changed, and no 420-second run was
started.

## Runs

- `dt=0.1 s`: 10 steps, 36 Newton iterations, PASS (existing smoke result).
- `dt=0.05 s`: 20 steps, 67 Newton iterations, PASS.
- `dt=0.025 s`: 40 steps, 126 Newton iterations, PASS.

The two new runs each completed in about 40 seconds including mesh loading and
output.

## Maximum common-time differences

`relative` for temperature is pointwise relative to the finer temperature.
For heat flow it is normalized by the maximum absolute finer-run flow over the
common times, avoiding division by a zero crossing.

| Pair (coarse vs fine) | Metric | Max absolute difference | Relative |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 0.1 vs 0.05 | GGG average T | 0.002394 K | 0.220% |
| 0.1 vs 0.05 | cold-stage average T | 0.087219 K | 8.100% |
| 0.1 vs 0.05 | sample average T | 0.088276 K | 8.197% |
| 0.1 vs 0.05 | support 1 flow | 8.472 uW | 15.144% |
| 0.1 vs 0.05 | support 2 flow | 10.633 uW | 17.264% |
| 0.1 vs 0.05 | switch flow | 19.603 uW | 10.724% |
| 0.05 vs 0.025 | GGG average T | 0.001668 K | 0.154% |
| 0.05 vs 0.025 | cold-stage average T | 0.011808 K | 1.109% |
| 0.05 vs 0.025 | sample average T | 0.011959 K | 1.123% |
| 0.05 vs 0.025 | support 1 flow | 2.841 uW | 5.350% |
| 0.05 vs 0.025 | support 2 flow | 3.315 uW | 5.688% |
| 0.05 vs 0.025 | switch flow | 0.913 uW | 0.499% |

For `0.05 vs 0.025`, cold-stage/sample differences are just above the proposed
`0.01 K` threshold, and both support flows are just above `5%`. GGG and switch
flow meet their absolute/relative criteria. The GGG difference is 19.1% of its
very small 0–1 s excursion, so that excursion-normalized percentage should not be
read without the 1.67 mK absolute scale.

## Decision

`dt=0.1 s` is not supported for the fast early interval by this comparison.
`dt=0.05 s` versus `0.025 s` is close but not yet within all provisional gates.
The next recommended convergence run is `dt=0.0125 s` over the same 1-second
interval. It was not started automatically.
