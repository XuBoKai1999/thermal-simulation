# Documentation note — discontinuous by_region IC in continuous P1 space

## Completed

- Identified and permanently documented a framework-wide numerical
  representation limitation: semantic constant `by_region` IC is supported, but
  discontinuous temperatures across touching regions cannot be represented exactly
  by the current continuous P1 transient temperature field.
- Recorded that the DG0 region-wise initial values are transferred into a shared
  continuous FE field, so interface smoothing and mesh-dependent initial gradients
  / heat-flux diagnostics may appear.
- Clarified that this is not material/contact physics, does not mean `by_region`
  is broken, and does not prevent ordinary perfect-contact transient solves.
- Added an initial-condition representation audit gate before the Step 9
  energy-balance audit. It requires comparison with intended ICs, t=0 regional
  statistics, stored-energy discrepancy, initial interface flux, mesh-refinement
  behavior, and use of the actual solver initial field in energy accounting.
- Recorded ADR01 as the known case: intended GGG 1 K adjacent to copper at 4 K.
  Its t=0 interface flux must not be interpreted as a mesh-independent engineering
  heat leak.
- Deliberately did not prescribe DG, L2 projection, smoothing, or another fix;
  the final treatment remains an evidence-driven future decision.
