## C. Remaining limitations

仍未支援：

- transient contact resistance
- general 3D zero-thickness contact
- radiation
- convection
- prescribed heat flux、total heat load、volumetric heat generation
- time-dependent BC
- heat switch
- anisotropic conductivity
- callable、region-mapped或外部檔案 initial field
- MPI dump及parallel cell-ID mapping
- 自動 solver/model selection與通用 time-loop runner
- 外部 CAD dependency 自動追蹤

目前 BC 仍只有 constant fixed temperature；未指定表面自然為零法向熱流。

## D. ADR readiness verdict

READY FOR ADR BASELINE

目前已具備 arbitrary 3D multi-region geometry、multiple semantic boundaries、uniform IC、constant或temperature-dependent材料、perfect shared-mesh contact、temperature/heat-flux fields、per-region statistics、selected-surface heat flow，以及既有 mesh/time-step convergence workflow。
