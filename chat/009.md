# ADR01 lateral-offset geometry revision

The Draft 0 interpretation was corrected so the ADR chain is no longer concentric with the hot
and cold plates. `cylinder_1`, `heat_switch`, `cylinder_2`, `ggg`, and `cylinder_3` are now recorded
as one coaxial group and share the placeholder centre `(-5, 0) mm`. The sample is centred near
`(+5, 0) mm` with a `6 × 6 mm` footprint. The supports were moved to `(0, ±9) mm` to avoid the
offset GGG/chain and sample while retaining their plate contacts.

The regenerated conformal mesh contains 2,506 nodes and 7,459 tagged elements. All 10 component
groups and 11 declared connection groups validate; there are no missing or extra component
contacts, split/overlapping volumes, duplicate nodes/elements, or isolated nodes. The geometry is
ready for another interactive Gmsh inspection and remains gated against thermal solving.
